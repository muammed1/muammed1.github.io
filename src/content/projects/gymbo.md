## Overview

Gymbo is documented as a centralized management platform for fitness centers. Public-safe sources
support engineering involvement across operational, financial, and administrative workflows, but
they do not establish an exact project title or feature-by-feature ownership.

## Engineering work

- Project sources describe a ledger-based financial model using debit and credit transactions.
- Project materials report 23 tables and 15 reporting views; schema definitions are unavailable.
- The documented reporting flow supports PDF and Excel exports.
- The case study describes Dahua biometric attendance-device integration with attendance workflows.
- Professional profile records support production delivery; deployment details remain undocumented.

## Public evidence boundary

The implementation is private. Public descriptions intentionally avoid unsupported adoption
counts, synchronization-latency guarantees, formal double-entry accounting claims, screen counts,
permission granularity, and audit-coverage guarantees.
